About

Voice, story,
improvisation.

Rebecca Curto is a singer from Northern Italy, currently studying at Berklee College of Music while continuing her musical activity in Italy. Her musical identity is rooted in storytelling, jazz tradition and intimate performance.

She grew up inspired by Italian singer-songwriters of the 1930s–60s, including Luigi Tenco, Fred Bongusto and Bruno Martino, alongside American jazz voices such as Sarah Vaughan and Anita O’Day. Gospel harmonies and improvisation complete her sound world.

“I focus my path on the search for sound and expressive purity, finding my ideal dimension in improvisation.”

Musical Language

Classic jazz.
Italian sensitivity.

Rebecca combines the elegance of classic jazz from the 1930s–60s with the intimate sensibility of Italian songwriting, creating a musical dialogue shaped by sincere nuances and narrative.
